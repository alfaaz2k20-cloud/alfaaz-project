## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `vigilance_and_error_detection`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`conscientiousness`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Proofreading Vigilance, Quality Control, & Error Detection Sensitivity.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Proofreading 3 exhibition display cards containing typographical and factual discrepancies (e.g. calendar errors, unrealistic units).
- **Claim Boundary:** Catching typographical errors on short placards does not guarantee error-free performance in high-volume clerical workflows.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Wilmot, M. P., & Ones, D. S. (2019). *A century of research on conscientiousness at work.* PNAS, 116, 23004–23010. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1908430116
- Dudley, N. M., et al. (2006). *A meta-analytic investigation of conscientiousness...* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 40–57. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.91.1.40

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
High conscientiousness involves vigilance in verifying details against known physical and calendar facts before signing off on final work. Conscientious reviewers inspect all placards and check only those containing concrete errors.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Inspection and checkbox toggle for each of the 3 placards (`placard_inspected`, `error_flagged`).
- Time taken before approving the ledger.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `placard_presented` (timestamp, placard_id, text)
- `checkbox_toggled` (timestamp, placard_id, is_checked)
- `ledger_approved` (timestamp, total_checked)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `error_detection_sensitivity`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{True Positives} + \text{True Negatives}}{\text{Total Placards (3)}}$
  - *Units:* Accuracy ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher accuracy indicates keen error spotting and absence of false alarms.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (3 discrete placard proofreading trials)

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Conscientiousness $\neq$ Blind Rejection:** Conscientious checking requires identifying real errors, not checking every card indiscriminately out of excessive suspicion.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Reading Speed / Visual Acuity:** Small font or complex units.
- **Domain Familiarity:** Spotting "450 Kilograms" for a wood plaque is commonsense, but "February 31st" tests calendar factuality.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Placard 1 is completely error-free (control card). Participants who blindly check all cards receive an accuracy penalty.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast text; standard HTML checkbox inputs navigable via Tab and Spacebar.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ A3 Convergence Hypothesis:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally with SJT conscientiousness items that involve catching administrative oversight or checking colleagues' work. Magnitude will be estimated empirically.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (A1, A2)
- **A3 $\leftrightarrow$ A1:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally; both measure accuracy and attention to detail.
- **A3 $\leftrightarrow$ A2:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally; both require spotting discrepancies against standard rules.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish sustained vigilance over hours of continuous inspection.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** 3 cards provide a clear Signal Detection / Accuracy framework ($N=3$).
- **Implementation Status:** `SPECIFICATION_COMPLETE`, but **NOT** `EXTRACTOR_IMPLEMENTATION_READY` because the client frontend currently logs only aggregate payload rather than discrete per-placard telemetry.
