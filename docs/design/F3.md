## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `empathy`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `context_sensitive_updating`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`empathy`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Dynamic Perspective-Taking & Social Context Updating.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Observes whether a participant updates their interpersonal response when environmental parameters shift, rather than perseverating on previously successful behavior.
- **Claim Boundary:** Task-specific acoustic context adjustment does not capture long-term interpersonal adaptation.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Healey, M. L., & Grossman, M. (2018). *Cognitive and Affective Perspective-Taking: Evidence for Shared and Dissociable Anatomical Substrates.* Frontiers in Neurology, 9, 491.
- Christian, M. S., Edwards, B. D., & Bradley, J. C. (2010). Personnel Psychology, 63, 83–117.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Empathetic attunement is not a static formula; what is supportive in one setting (e.g. an empty rehearsal hall) becomes unhelpful in another (e.g. a crowded recital). The candidate must recognize that partner needs have changed when the room acoustic context shifts and adjust their behavior accordingly.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Dynamic slider adjustments across room transition phases.
- Pacing of recalibration following room context announcement.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `context_transition_triggered` (timestamp, old_context, new_context)
- `adjustment_initiated` (timestamp)
- `setting_finalized` (timestamp, final_value, target_value)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `context_updating_efficiency`
  - *Formula:* $1 - \frac{|\text{final\_setting} - \text{new\_target}|}{\text{max\_error}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher value indicates prompt recalibration to new context.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Empathy $\neq$ Indiscriminate Accommodation:** The candidate must not simply keep moving the dial blindly; the goal is alignment with the new environmental reality.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Perceptual Processing Speed:** Detecting room change indicator quickly.
- **Motor Control:** Precision slider positioning.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Requires consecutive shifts where one shift demands reversal of the previous adjustment.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Visual and auditory cues for context change; step navigation supported.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ F3 Convergence Hypothesis:** Low-to-moderate positive correlation. SJT presents static situational trade-offs; F3 tests live updating.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (F1, F2)
- **F3 $\leftrightarrow$ F1:** F1 measures baseline attunement; F3 measures post-shift recalibration.
- **F3 $\leftrightarrow$ F2:** F2 tests ambiguity handling; F3 tests dynamic context changes.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure empathy in unstructured human conversation or emotional conflict.

- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
