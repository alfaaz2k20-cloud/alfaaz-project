# Mini-Game Design Sheet: A1 — Classification
**World:** The Archive (`W2`)  
**Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `rule_governed_sorting`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `conscientiousness`
- **Facet:** `rule_governed_sorting`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `conscientiousness`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Rule-Governed Categorization & Attentiveness to Detail
- **Honesty Note:** Structured archival sorting task evaluating adherence to multi-attribute classification criteria under neutral pacing.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate catalogs historical cultural documents into categorized archival drawers according to era, medium, and preservation protocol.
- **Trial Structure:** 6 document filing trials with multi-criteria metadata cards.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Reading structured metadata tags; differentiated from A2/A3 by testing standard rule application without deliberate exceptions or post-hoc auditing.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Items with obvious single-category tags interspersed with multi-attribute items.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `document_opened`
- `metadata_inspected`
- `rule_guide_viewed`
- `category_selected`
- `document_filed`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `classification_rule_adherence_rate`
- **Formula:** count(correctly classified documents) / total_documents
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Higher ratio indicates meticulous application of classification rubrics.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Categorical correctness normalized by item count.

### Feature: `verification_duration_ratio`
- **Formula:** total_time_reviewing_rules_and_metadata / total_task_duration
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Process metric capturing deliberation and pre-decision verification.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Proportional temporal investment.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `4`
- **Expected Observations:** `6`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** All documents filed in under 2 seconds without inspecting metadata.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** High contrast text tags, keyboard drag/drop or numeric slot assignment (1-4).

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Fewer than 4 documents processed before exiting. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
