## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Rule-Governed Categorization, Orderliness, & Attentiveness to Detail.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Structured archival sorting task evaluating adherence to multi-attribute classification criteria under unhurried pacing.
- **Claim Boundary:** Task-specific filing accuracy does not measure broad occupational dependability, lifetime work ethic, or moral integrity.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Wilmot, M. P., & Ones, D. S. (2019). *A century of research on conscientiousness at work.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 116, 23004–23010. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1908430116
- Dudley, N. M., Orvis, K. A., Lebiecki, J. E., & Cortina, J. M. (2006). *A meta-analytic investigation of conscientiousness in the prediction of job performance: Examining narrow traits.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 40–57. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.91.1.40

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Conscientiousness at work manifests in meticulous, rule-consistent execution of detailed tasks. When categorizing historical documents with overlapping attributes, conscientious individuals carefully inspect metadata tags and verify ambiguous criteria against the archival guide, resulting in high categorization fidelity.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Document inspection and category selection across 6 trials (`document_filed`, `item_sorted`, `is_correct`).
- Time spent reviewing metadata cards and shelf rules modal (`rule_guide_viewed`, `guide_viewed`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `document_presented` (timestamp, doc_id, index)
- `guide_viewed` (timestamp, doc_id)
- `item_sorted` (timestamp, doc_id, choice, is_correct, dwell_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `classification_rule_adherence_rate`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\sum \text{is\_correct}}{\text{total\_documents\_filed}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates rigorous adherence to classification rubrics.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **6** (6 discrete document sorting trials)
- **Feature 2:** `verification_duration_ratio`
  - *Formula:* $\min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{len(guide\_events)} \times 3000.0}{\max(\text{total\_dwell}, 1000.0)}\right)$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio captures deliberate pre-decision rule verification.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **6** (aggregated dwell across 6 trials)

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Conscientiousness $\neq$ Guide-Open Count:** Meticulous work is reflected in outcome accuracy and proportional deliberation. Repeatedly opening and closing the guide without reading does not indicate conscientiousness.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Working Memory / Reading Comprehension:** Complex metadata tags could penalize participants with lower verbal memory. Controlled by keeping tags short and keeping the guide accessible at all times without penalty.
- **Processing Speed:** Pacing is untimed (up to generous 35s ceiling).

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- 6 items contain a balanced mixture of obvious single-attribute items (control) and multi-attribute items requiring guide consultation.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast text cards; category selection via numeric keys (1–3); screen reader labels provided for each shelf.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ A1 Convergence Hypothesis:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally. Both evaluate attention to procedural standards and diligence, though the SJT assesses organizational responsibility and policy trade-offs while A1 assesses micro-level cognitive-procedural sorting precision. Behavioral tasks often correlate only weakly with declarative SJT items; the magnitude of association is not pre-specified and will be estimated empirically.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (A2, A3)
- **A1 $\leftrightarrow$ A2:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally. A1 evaluates standard rule adherence across multi-attribute folios; A2 evaluates protocol selection for damaged/anomalous folios.
- **A1 $\leftrightarrow$ A3:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally. A3 evaluates proofreading vigilance.
- **Internal Structure:** Positive inter-task correlation expected directionally across all three games, reflecting shared common variance in detail-orientation and quality focus, while separating task-specific variance (sorting vs exception detection vs text proofreading).

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish long-term attendance, organizational citizenship, or ethical dependability.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** 6 distinct document filing trials provide adequate observation count ($\ge 4$ required).
- **Control:** Full access to rules avoids pure memory confound.
