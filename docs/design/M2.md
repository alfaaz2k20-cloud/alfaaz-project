# Mini-Game Design Sheet: M2 — Voluntary Persistence (The Courtesy Sleeves)
**World:** The Repetition (`W7`)  
**Target Parameter:** `motivation`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `voluntary_persistence_beyond_minimum`  
**Measurement Status:** `READY FOR SPECIFICATION CONFIRMATION`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `motivation`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `voluntary_persistence_beyond_minimum`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`motivation`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Autonomous Persistence, Voluntary Goal Extension, & Disengagement Flexibility.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates whether a candidate voluntarily prepares 1 to 3 additional courtesy sleeves after explicitly completing the mandatory task and receiving explicit, unpenalized permission to exit.
- **Claim Boundary:** Stamping extra digital courtesy sleeves does not capture total volunteer dedication or self-determination across real organizational projects.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Brandstätter, V., & Bernecker, K. (2022). *Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit.* Annual Review of Psychology, 73, 271–299. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
In volunteer organizations, activities frequently involve a stated minimum requirement followed by voluntary opportunities to contribute extra support if personal energy and interest permit. The candidate is told the 3 mandatory invitations are finished, and is invited to stamp up to 3 optional extra sleeves or proceed immediately.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Number of optional courtesy sleeves stamped (0, 1, 2, or 3).
- Decision to click "Proceed" immediately vs click "+ Seal Extra Courtesy Sleeve".

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `optional_phase_started` (timestamp, mandatory_completed: 3, max_optional: 3)
- `optional_envelope_stamped` (timestamp, current_optional_count: 1..3)
- `optional_phase_concluded` (timestamp, final_optional_count, exit_reason: "proceed_clicked" | "all_completed")

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `voluntary_extension_count`
  - *Formula:* $\text{count}(\text{optional sleeves stamped})$
  - *Units:* Integer (0 to 3)
  - *Direction:* Higher count indicates voluntary extension of effort beyond the stated minimum.
- **Feature 2:** `persistence_censored_ratio`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{voluntary\_extension\_count}}{\text{max\_optional (3)}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Right-censored ratio. Stopping at 0 is explicitly treated as neutral baseline compliance, NOT as failure or low motivation.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Motivation $\neq$ Inability to Disengage:** Stamping extra sleeves is optional; choosing to proceed after the required minimum is completely valid and must NEVER be treated as poor motivation or penalized as LOW evidence.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Time Constraints:** Candidate may have a meeting or urgent personal schedule constraint and click Proceed despite high underlying volunteer motivation.
- **Impression Management:** Believing that stamping extra sleeves is "expected" by recruiters.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Explicit notice: *"The mandatory 3 invitations are already sealed. You may proceed at any time."*
- Prominent, unpenalized exit button active throughout.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast button labels; keyboard Tab/Enter support.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ M2 Convergence Hypothesis:** Moderate positive correlation with SJT items on volunteering for extra tasks.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (M1, M3)
- **M2 $\leftrightarrow$ M1:** M1 captures baseline compliance; M2 captures discretionary persistence.
- **M2 $\leftrightarrow$ M3:** M3 introduces persistence without extrinsic feedback.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure lifelong volunteer tenacity or resilience against burnout.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Clear 4-level voluntary gradient (0, 1, 2, 3 sleeves) with explicit exit architecture.
- **Right-Censoring Handled:** Maximum 3 units prevents endless loop; baseline 0 is neutral.
