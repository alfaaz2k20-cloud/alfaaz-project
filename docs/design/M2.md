# Mini-Game Design Sheet: M2 — Optional Continuation
**World:** The Repetition (`W7`)  
**Target Parameter:** `motivation`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `voluntary_persistence`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `motivation`
- **Facet:** `voluntary_persistence`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `motivation`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Voluntary Task Persistence Beyond Stated Minimum
- **Honesty Note:** Measures spontaneous decision to continue repetitive cataloguing when explicitly told they are free to proceed or stop without penalty.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** After completing the mandatory 3 units, the UI neutrally informs: 'Minimum completed. You may continue formatting additional records or conclude this world.'
- **Trial Structure:** Up to 5 optional units with honest neutral choice prompt after each unit.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** None; completely transparent choice prompt ('Finish & Continue' vs 'Format Another').

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Stopping at minimum is treated as neutral, valid baseline behavior, never penalized.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `optional_prompt_displayed`
- `choice_logged`
- `optional_unit_started`
- `optional_unit_finished`
- `ceiling_reached`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `optional_units_completed`
- **Formula:** count(completed optional units)
- **Units:** units (0-5)
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Voluntary persistence on unglamorous organizational tasks. Note: Right-censored at ceiling.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Count of discrete voluntary tasks completed.

### Feature: `voluntary_duration_ms`
- **Formula:** sum(time spent on optional units)
- **Units:** ms
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Total voluntary time investment in routine support.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Continuous duration metric.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `1`
- **Expected Observations:** `5`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `40000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Immediate crash or invalid state.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Accessible button prompt with direct keyboard focus.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** No choice registered. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
