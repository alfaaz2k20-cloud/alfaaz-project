## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `motivation`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `baseline_task_diligence`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`motivation`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Baseline Task Engagement, Diligence, & Pacing Regularity.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates steady, attentive completion of an explicitly required minimal obligation (3 wax seals on formal event invitations).
- **Claim Boundary:** Completing 3 digital wax seals does not measure multi-month dedication, altruistic self-sacrifice, or broad volunteer motivation.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Brandstätter, V., & Bernecker, K. (2022). *Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit.* Annual Review of Psychology, 73, 271–299. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Dependable motivation begins with fulfilling baseline commitments attentively and consistently. Participants are asked to complete exactly 3 invitations; pacing consistency and deliberate application reflect baseline diligence.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Application of 3 wax seals across sequential invitations (`seal_applied`).
- Inter-trial response latency and pacing regularity (`latency_per_seal_ms`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `envelope_presented` (timestamp, envelope_id: 1..3, recipient_name)
- `seal_applied` (timestamp, envelope_id, latency_ms)
- `batch_completed` (timestamp, total_completed: 3)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `baseline_completion_adherence`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{completed\_required\_units}}{\text{required\_units (3)}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (1.0 = full baseline completion)
  - *Direction:* Binary/Ratio indicator of obligation fulfillment.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (3 discrete mandatory envelope sealing trials)
- **Feature 2:** `pacing_consistency_cv`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{Standard Deviation}(\text{latencies})}{\text{Mean}(\text{latencies})}$
  - *Units:* Coefficient of Variation
  - *Direction:* Lower variability indicates steady, self-regulated pacing.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (latencies across 3 envelopes)

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Motivation $\neq$ Longest Persistence or Speed Racing:** Rapid clicking to rush through does not indicate high motivation; consistent and careful pacing is the operational target.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Motor / Device Differences:** Mouse clicking speed.
- **Boredom / Fatigue:** Being the 7th world in the battery.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Clear, unhurried instructions stating exactly 3 envelopes are required.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Keyboard Enter/Spacebar triggers seal stamping; audible or visual stamp confirmation.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ M1 Convergence Hypothesis:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally with SJT motivation items focusing on fulfilling baseline commitments. Behavioral tasks often correlate only weakly with declarative SJT items; the magnitude of association is not pre-specified and will be estimated empirically.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (M2, M3)
- **M1 $\leftrightarrow$ M2:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* M1 captures baseline compliance; M2 captures discretionary persistence.
- **M1 $\leftrightarrow$ M3:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* M3 tests persistence under reduced external feedback.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure lifelong volunteer passion, ideological commitment, or resistance to severe burnout.
