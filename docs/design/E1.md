# Mini-Game Design Sheet: E1 — Rule Shift Adaptation (The Ceramic Mosaic)
**World:** The Shifting Grid (`W4`)  
**Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `behavioral_adaptation_under_rule_change`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `behavioral_adaptation_under_rule_change`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`emotional_agility`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Regulatory Flexibility, Cognitive Flexibility, & Task Switching.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates behavioral recovery of response accuracy and speed when sorting rules transition mid-task from color-matching to shape-matching.
- **Claim Boundary:** Task-switching adaptation in a card sorting game does not measure internal emotional feelings, mood regulation, or recovery from severe personal trauma.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Bonanno, G. A., & Burton, C. L. (2013). *Regulatory Flexibility: An Individual Differences Perspective on Coping and Emotion Regulation.* Perspectives on Psychological Science, 8, 591–612. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691613504116
- Koechlin, E., Ody, C., & Kouneiher, F. (2003). *The architecture of cognitive control in the human prefrontal cortex.* Science, 302(5648), 1181–1185. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1088545 *(Neurocognitive model of hierarchical control; provides theoretical framing for contextual adaptation but does NOT validate E1 or any Alfaaz task).*

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
When situational demands shift unexpectedly, individuals with high adaptability recover performance quickly: they suppress previously reinforced sorting habits, adopt the new criterion, and minimize perseverative errors.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Sorting choice across 6 trials before and after the rule change at trial 3 (`tile_sorted`, `rule_active`, `is_correct`).
- Response latency on post-shift trials (`shift_switch_cost_ms`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `tile_presented` (timestamp, tile_id, color, shape, active_rule)
- `bin_selected` (timestamp, choice_bin, is_correct, latency_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `post_shift_accuracy_recovery`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\sum \text{is\_correct (trials 4–6)}}{\text{total post-shift trials (3)}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates swift suppression of perseveration and accurate adoption of new rule.
- **Feature 2:** `switch_cost_latency_ratio`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{t(\text{trial 4 latency})}{\text{mean}(t(\text{trials 1–3 latency}))}$
  - *Units:* Ratio
  - *Direction:* Values closer to 1.0 indicate minimal disruption from the shift.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Emotional Agility $\neq$ Direct Internal Emotional Measurement:** The task measures behavioral adaptation and flexibility under change, not subjective emotional experience or physiological distress.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Explicit Instruction Following:** The current client implementation prominently displays a banner: `Active Rule: Match by Shape`. This turns the test into reading compliance rather than adaptive rule-induction.
- **Visual Processing Speed:** Rapid tile categorization.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Requires implicit rule shifting (or unannounced change detected via feedback) to avoid simple explicit instruction following.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast color and shape identifiers; keyboard number keys for bin selection.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ E1 Convergence Hypothesis:** Low-to-moderate positive correlation. SJT assesses strategic adaptability across complex scenarios; E1 assesses micro-level behavioral flexibility.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (E2, E3)
- **E1 $\leftrightarrow$ E2:** E1 tests procedural shifting; E2 tests response to unplanned real-world interruption.
- **E1 $\leftrightarrow$ E3:** E3 tests equilibrium under aesthetic ambiguity.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure internal stress, affective stability, or neuroticism.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Only 6 trials total (3 pre-shift, 3 post-shift).
- **Design Confound:** Explicit banner announces rule change, confounding cognitive flexibility with basic reading.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on whether the rule shift is explicit (instruction following) or implicit (feedback-based shift discovery) and expansion of trial block.
