# Mini-Game Design Sheet: E1 — Rule Shift
**World:** The Shifting Grid (`W4`)  
**Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `cognitive_flexibility_under_rule_change`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `emotional_agility`
- **Facet:** `cognitive_flexibility_under_rule_change`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `emotional_agility`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Behavioral Flexibility & Set-Shifting (Wisconsin Card Sort Analog)
- **Honesty Note:** Measures adaptation when established sorting rules (color vs shape vs glyph) silently change.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate sorts geometric cultural symbols into quadrants. After establishing a stable sorting pattern, the matching criterion silently shifts.
- **Trial Structure:** 18 continuous sorting trials with 2 unannounced dimension shifts.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Rapid classification motor speed. Differentiated by pure rule reversal vs setback recovery (E2) or condition disruption (E3).

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Initial 6 trials established with unambiguous feedback.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `symbol_presented`
- `quadrant_selected`
- `feedback_displayed`
- `rule_shift_triggered`
- `perseverative_error_logged`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `perseverative_error_count`
- **Formula:** count(incorrect choices adhering to obsolete rule immediately post-shift)
- **Units:** count
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Lower perseveration indicates agile mental flexibility and rapid detachment from obsolete assumptions.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Categorical error classification.

### Feature: `trials_to_criterion_post_shift`
- **Formula:** count(trials until 3 consecutive correct post-shift)
- **Units:** trials
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Fewer trials indicates swift recalibration to new operating reality.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Convergence speed metric.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `12`
- **Expected Observations:** `18`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Random spam clicking without regard to feedback.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** High contrast shape/color/symbol dual encoding; keyboard keys 1-4.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Less than 12 trials completed or skipped during first shift. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
