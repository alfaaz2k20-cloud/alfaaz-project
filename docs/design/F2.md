# Mini-Game Design Sheet: F2 — Ambiguous Cue
**World:** The Frequency (`W1`)  
**Target Parameter:** `empathy`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `clarification_under_ambiguity`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `empathy`
- **Facet:** `clarification_under_ambiguity`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `empathy`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Social Clarification Seeking under Ambiguity
- **Honesty Note:** Analog of communicative inquiry when social signals are low-fidelity.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Partner gives an ambiguous cue ('something feels slightly off in the balance') while signal is within operational bounds. Candidate can probe for clarification, refine subtly, or maintain state.
- **Trial Structure:** 3 trials with varying cue ambiguity (clear error, ambiguous preference, false alarm).

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Reading speed of short prompt; differentiated from F1 by offering explicit clarification action vs raw slider tuning.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Trial where signal is objectively distorted and partner cue is clear: clarification is inefficient, immediate adjustment is appropriate.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `cue_displayed`
- `clarification_requested`
- `slider_adjusted`
- `decision_submitted`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `clarification_vs_assumption_ratio`
- **Formula:** count(clarification_requests on ambiguous trials) / count(ambiguous_trials)
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Higher ratio reflects willingness to verify partner perspective before acting on ambiguous signals.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Categorical choice under specific informational ambiguity condition.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `2`
- **Expected Observations:** `3`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `30000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Zero responses registered or timeout without interaction.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Full screen-reader semantic buttons; keyboard selectable.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Trial skipped or timed out before choice selection. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
