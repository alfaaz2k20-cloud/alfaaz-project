# Mini-Game Design Sheet: F2 — Ambiguous Cue Resolution (The Gathering Voices)

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `empathy`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `cognitive_perspective_taking`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`empathy`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Cognitive Perspective-Taking & Interpersonal Disambiguation.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Laboratory analog assessing whether an individual clarifies ambiguous interpersonal preferences before acting, rather than projecting assumptions.
- **Claim Boundary:** Selecting a clarification response in a vignette does not demonstrate generalized interpersonal sensitivity in naturalistic group dynamics.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Healey, M. L., & Grossman, M. (2018). *Cognitive and Affective Perspective-Taking: Evidence for Shared and Dissociable Anatomical Substrates.* Frontiers in Neurology, 9, 491.
- Christian, M. S., Edwards, B. D., & Bradley, J. C. (2010). *Situational Judgment Tests: Constructs Assessed and a Meta-Analysis.* Personnel Psychology, 63, 83–117.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
In real-world collaboration, communication cues are frequently noisy or ambiguous. Individuals higher in cognitive perspective-taking recognize when information is insufficient to deduce another's comfort or intent, choosing to clarify kindly rather than imposing arbitrary changes or ignoring the signal.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Option selection in response to ambiguous partner sound feedback: clarifying (`F2_ASK`) vs test adjustment (`F2_TEST`) vs deferral (`F2_HOLD`).
- Decision latency.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `ambiguity_presented` (timestamp, prompt_id)
- `option_selected` (timestamp, option_id, latency_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `clarification_preference_score`
  - *Formula:* Categorical mapping (Clarification = 1.0, Modest Test = 0.5, Passive Hold = 0.0)
  - *Units:* Score (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher indicates propensity to verify partner preferences actively.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Empathy $\neq$ Indiscriminate Accommodation:** Clarifying what is needed preserves partner autonomy; immediately yielding without knowing what the partner wants is confusion, not empathy.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Reading Ability & Comprehension:** Options require verbal parsing.
- **Compliance / Social Desirability:** "Ask with kindness" sounds overtly prosocial.
- **Alternative Explanation:** Candidate chose the option because it contains polite phrasing ("kindness"), not due to genuine cognitive perspective-taking.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs trials where clarifying is inefficient/annoying because the signal was already explicit.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast text options; full keyboard navigation (numeric 1–3).

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ F2 Convergence Hypothesis:** Moderate correlation expected because F2 closely resembles an SJT mini-vignette format. Higher overlap with SJT than F1 or F3.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (F1, F3)
- **F2 $\leftrightarrow$ F1:** F1 measures fast perceptual latency; F2 measures deliberate cognitive disambiguation.
- **F2 $\leftrightarrow$ F3:** F3 tests ongoing adaptation across shifts.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish spontaneous empathy in non-verbal or high-stress contexts.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Currently implemented as a single 1-shot multiple-choice question.
- **Method Contamination:** Effectively duplicates the SJT format within a game world rather than providing game-based behavioral observation.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on whether F2 remains an interactive dialogue tree with multiple steps or is converted into an active behavioral task.
