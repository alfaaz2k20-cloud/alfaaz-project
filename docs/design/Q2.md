# Mini-Game Design Sheet: Q2 — Anomaly Exploration (The Uncataloged Seal)
**World:** The Hidden Gallery (`W5`)  
**Target Parameter:** `curiosity`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `investigation_under_uncertainty`  
**Measurement Status:** `READY FOR SPECIFICATION CONFIRMATION`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `curiosity`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `investigation_under_uncertainty`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`curiosity`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Information-Seeking Under Uncertainty, Epistemic Anomaly Detection.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Inspecting an enigmatic uncataloged wax seal to reveal its hidden inscription rather than skipping over it.
- **Claim Boundary:** Inspecting an unexplained seal in a web mini-game does not establish persistent investigative behavior in professional life.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Gottlieb, J., Oudeyer, P.-Y., Lopes, M., & Baranes, A. (2013). *Information-seeking, curiosity, and attention...* Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 17, 585–593.
- Kidd, C., & Hayden, B. Y. (2015). *The Psychology and Neuroscience of Curiosity.* Neuron, 88, 449–460.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Curiosity is triggered by information gaps or unexpected anomalies. When encountering an uncataloged artifact with partial markings, individuals high in curiosity invest time to uncover and understand the anomaly rather than ignoring it.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Voluntary click to inspect the uncataloged seal (`seal_inspected`).
- Dwell time on the revealed inscription history (`seal_inspection_dwell_ms`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `artifact_presented` (timestamp, artifact_id)
- `detail_magnified` / `seal_inspected` (timestamp)
- `task_concluded` (timestamp, total_dwell_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `anomaly_investigation_dwell`
  - *Formula:* $\min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{inspection\_dwell\_ms}}{10000.0}\right)$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates deliberate engagement with unexplained historical details.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Curiosity $\neq$ Click Count:** Clicking and closing immediately does not constitute epistemic investigation.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Time Pressure / Candidate Hurry:** Candidates eager to finish may skip regardless of baseline curiosity.
- **Visual Legibility:** Clean contrast required.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Clearly marked standard catalog entries alongside the uncataloged anomaly.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Text alternative for seal imagery; keyboard accessible magnification button.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ Q2 Convergence Hypothesis:** Moderate positive correlation with curiosity questions regarding continuous learning.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (Q1, Q3)
- **Q2 $\leftrightarrow$ Q1:** High correlation expected; both measure voluntary inspection of extra cultural material.
- **Q2 $\leftrightarrow$ Q3:** Q3 builds on discovered knowledge.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure scientific problem-solving or formal research methodology.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Discrete voluntary inspection opportunity with measurable dwell.
- **Status:** `READY FOR SPECIFICATION CONFIRMATION`.
