## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Curiosity is triggered by information gaps or unexpected anomalies. When encountering an uncataloged artifact with partial markings, individuals high in curiosity invest time to uncover and understand the anomaly rather than ignoring it.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Voluntary click to inspect the uncataloged seal (`seal_inspected`).
- Dwell time on the revealed inscription history (`seal_inspection_dwell_ms`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `artifact_presented` (timestamp, artifact_id)
- `detail_magnified` / `seal_inspected` (timestamp)
- `task_concluded` (timestamp, total_dwell_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `anomaly_investigation_dwell`
  - *Formula:* $\min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{inspection\_dwell\_ms}}{10000.0}\right)$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates deliberate engagement with unexplained historical details.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **1**
  - *CRITICAL DOCUMENTATION NOTE:* **`SINGLE_OBSERVATION`**. A single seal inspection ($N=1$) is susceptible to accidental clicking or hurried skipping. While valid as an experimental indicator, single-observation features provide minimal psychometric information on their own and require multi-item sampling for robust individual measurement.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Curiosity $\neq$ Click Count:** Clicking and closing immediately does not constitute epistemic investigation.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Time Pressure / Candidate Hurry:** Candidates eager to finish may skip regardless of baseline curiosity.
- **Visual Legibility:** Clean contrast required.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
