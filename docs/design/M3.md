## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Autonomous Motivation, Intrinsic Self-Regulation, & Persistence Under Extinction / Low Saliency.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates whether repetitive effort is sustained when visual progress animations, audio chimes, and extrinsic confirmations are stripped away.
- **Claim Boundary:** Continuing a brief unreinforced web task does not prove unshakeable self-motivation in long-term non-profit projects.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Brandstätter, V., & Bernecker, K. (2022). *Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit.* Annual Review of Psychology, 73, 271–299. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
In real-world non-profit operations, volunteers frequently perform necessary routine duties late in the evening without cheering crowds or immediate feedback. Autonomous motivation is demonstrated when work quality and follow-through persist even when extrinsic reinforcement is absent.

## 5. Construct-Preserving Design Alternative (Documentation Only — Do NOT Implement)
To preserve construct integrity and mechanical continuity with M1 and M2, M3 must **continue the repetitive stamping/sealing mechanics of M2 under reduced/non-salient feedback**, completely abandoning the flawed checklist/verification format:
- **Mechanical Continuity:** The candidate continues stamping/sealing invitation folios (same motor/procedural mechanic as M1 and M2).
- **Explicit Stated Minimum:** Candidate is instructed to prepare exactly 2 formal archival folios for the quiet collection.
- **Voluntary Continuation Beyond Minimum:** After completing the 2 required folios, candidate is told:
  > *"The required 2 folios are complete. Up to 3 additional folios are available on the bench. You may seal extra folios, or click Complete Part 3 to proceed at any time."*
- **Stopping at Minimum is Neutral:** Exiting immediately upon completing the 2 mandatory units is treated as standard baseline fulfillment, never penalized or labeled low.
- **Honest Candidate-Facing Wording:** Button is labeled *"Complete Part 3 &rarr;"* (or *"Complete Workshop Task &rarr;"*). It is **never** labeled "Finalize Assessment" or "Submit Session" (which is false in counterbalanced Latin square order where World 7 appears first in Rows 6 and 9). Zero claims of silent background saving; no implication that work is submitted or used unless literally true.
- **No Hidden Construct Disclosure:** No evaluative banners, no psychological labels.
- **No False Feedback / Error:** The interface never feigns errors, artificial crashes, or lost progress.

## 6. Documented Confound Analysis
- **Primary Confound:** Reduced or non-salient feedback is heavily confounded with:
  1. *Time-on-Task & Fatigue:* World 7 occurs after multiple intensive tasks;
  2. *Altered Task Salience:* Candidates may interpret the sudden absence of celebratory visual flourishes as an interface lag or a sign that the task is trivial/unimportant, leading to premature exit that reflects rational effort-conservation rather than low intrinsic motivation.

## 7. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `mandatory_folio_completed` (timestamp, folio_id)
- `optional_folio_completed` (timestamp, folio_id)
- `part3_completed` (timestamp, total_optional_completed)

## 8. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `reduced_feedback_persistence_count`
  - *Formula:* Count of optional folios completed beyond the mandatory 2.
  - *Units:* Count ($N \in [0, 3]$)
  - *Direction:* Higher count indicates intrinsic motivation to continue repetitive contribution under extinction/low-saliency conditions.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **Up to 3** optional units ($N \in [0, 3]$).

## 9. Important Construct Boundary
- **Motivation $\neq$ Obsessive Compulsion:** Candidates should stop when their self-determined contribution goal is reached; ending the task is healthy self-regulation, never penalized.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions & Honesty Guardrail
- **Honesty Guardrail (Audit Finding & Blocker):**
  1. *Deceptive Auto-Save Claim:* The previous draft suggestion claiming *"work saves silently in background"* is **flagged as a blocker** because no background document auto-save exists and claiming auto-saving to candidates would be deceptive.
  2. *False Finalization Wording under Latin Square Counterbalancing:* In the client UI, M3 displays *"Click Finalize Assessment to submit your session"* and a button labeled *"Finalize Assessment &rarr;"*. Because R4 14-row Latin square counterbalancing is active (assigning The Repetition to positions 0 through 6, including Position 0 as the very first world in Rows 6 and 9), telling candidates they are "submitting their session" is factually false whenever The Repetition is not the final world.
- **Proposed Honest Replacement Wording (Design Sheet Proposal Only — No Code Changes):**  
  - *Screen Instruction Copy:*
    > *"Part 3: The Evening Threshold. Prepare the 2 formal archival folios for the quiet collection. When you are ready, click Complete Part 3 to proceed."*
  - *Button Label:*
    > *"Complete Part 3 &rarr;"*  
    *(Replaces "Finalize Assessment &rarr;")*
  - *Low-Feedback Clarification:*
    > *"Visual celebration animations are subdued for this closing task, but your folio completions are actively recorded. You may proceed at your own pace."*
