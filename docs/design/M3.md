## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `motivation`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `autonomous_self_regulation_without_extrinsic_feedback`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`motivation`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Autonomous Motivation, Intrinsic Self-Regulation, & Persistence Under Extinction / Low Reward.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates whether effort is sustained when visual progress animations, audio chimes, and immediate extrinsic confirmations are stripped away.
- **Claim Boundary:** Continuing a brief unreinforced web task does not prove unshakeable self-motivation in long-term non-profit projects.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Brandstätter, V., & Bernecker, K. (2022). *Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit.* Annual Review of Psychology, 73, 271–299. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
In real-world non-profit operations, volunteers frequently perform necessary routine duties late in the evening without cheering crowds or immediate feedback. Autonomous motivation is demonstrated when work quality and follow-through persist even when extrinsic reinforcement is absent.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Voluntary continuation and steady execution when visual celebration cues are removed.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `reduced_feedback_phase_started` (timestamp)
- `repetition_unit_executed` (timestamp, unit_id)
- `task_exit_triggered` (timestamp, total_units)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `unreinforced_persistence_count`
  - *Formula:* Count of optional units performed in the absence of feedback animations.
  - *Units:* Count
  - *Direction:* Higher count indicates intrinsic task engagement independent of external praise.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (checklist items in current client)

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Motivation $\neq$ Obsessive Compulsion:** Candidates should stop when their self-determined goal is reached; ending the session is healthy self-regulation.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Session Fatigue:** World 7 is at the very end of the recruitment session.
- **Confusion / UI Glitch Attribution:** Absence of feedback might lead some users to believe the site has frozen.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions & Honesty Guardrail
- **Honesty Guardrail (Audit Finding & Blocker):** The previous draft suggestion claiming *"work saves silently in background"* is **flagged as a blocker** because no background document auto-save exists and claiming auto-saving to candidates would be deceptive.
- **Proposed Honest Replacement Wording (Design Sheet Proposal Only):**  
  > *"The visual animations are subdued for this closing inspection, but your checklist responses are actively recorded. You may finalize at your own pace."*

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Clear textual status indicator confirming backend receipt of events.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ M3 Convergence Hypothesis:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally. Magnitude will be estimated empirically.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (M1, M2)

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure resistance to chronic organizational apathy or burnout.
