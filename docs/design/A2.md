# Mini-Game Design Sheet: A2 — Exception Handling
**World:** The Archive (`W2`)  
**Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `diligence_under_ambiguity`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `conscientiousness`
- **Facet:** `diligence_under_ambiguity`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `conscientiousness`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Exception Identification and Protocol Discipline
- **Honesty Note:** Evaluates handling of ambiguous, damaged, or rule-conflicting records.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate encounters rare archival items that do not neatly fit standard categories (e.g. bilingual fragment, dual-period artifact). Option to flag for secondary review or force-fit.
- **Trial Structure:** 4 trials (2 standard items, 2 genuine edge cases requiring exception protocol).

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Cognitive judgment of boundary conditions; sibling A1 tests standard rules, A3 tests retrospective verification.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Items that appear complex but have an explicit fallback clause in the archival rule sheet.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `edge_case_presented`
- `rule_exception_consulted`
- `quarantine_or_flag_action`
- `decision_logged`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `exception_flagging_precision`
- **Formula:** count(genuine exceptions properly flagged) / total_edge_cases
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Higher precision reflects systematic handling of boundary conditions without reckless forcing.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Rule-criterion compliance score.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `3`
- **Expected Observations:** `4`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** No actions taken or immediate random filing.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Full keyboard navigation and screen reader labels for metadata flags.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Less than 3 trials completed. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
