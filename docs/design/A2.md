# Mini-Game Design Sheet: A2 — Exception Handling (The Fragile Leaf)
**World:** The Archive (`W2`)  
**Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `exception_resolution`  
**Measurement Status:** `READY FOR SPECIFICATION CONFIRMATION (SINGLE-ITEM AUDIT DECISION)`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `exception_resolution`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`conscientiousness`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Exception Management, Protocol Adherence Under Uncertainty, & Thoroughness.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Observes whether a participant correctly identifies damaged or ambiguous material requiring protective quarantine rather than forcing it into a standard category.
- **Claim Boundary:** A single archival handling decision does not guarantee broad workplace risk management or diligence under sustained ambiguity.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Wilmot, M. P., & Ones, D. S. (2019). *A century of research on conscientiousness at work.* PNAS, 116, 23004–23010.
- Dudley, N. M., et al. (2006). *A meta-analytic investigation of conscientiousness...* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 40–57.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Conscientious professionals do not blindly force ambiguous or damaged records into routine categories where they might suffer harm; they flag anomalies, check condition stamps, and apply specialized preservation protocols.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Selection of specialized quarantine/preservation handling vs hasty routine accessioning (`decision_logged`, `exception_resolved`, `is_correct`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `exception_presented` (timestamp, item_id, condition_tags)
- `decision_logged` / `exception_resolved` (timestamp, choice, is_correct)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `exception_flagging_precision`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\sum \text{is\_correct}}{\text{total\_exception\_decisions}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher score indicates correct identification and protocol application for anomalous materials.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Conscientiousness $\neq$ Inflexible Bureaucracy:** Recognizing an exception requires discerning when the standard rule does not fit, balancing care with pragmatism.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Reading Ability:** Condition note parsing.
- **Risk Aversion:** High caution could lead to flagging everything as damaged if multiple items existed.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Standard non-damaged folios contrasted with the water-damaged leaf.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Textual description of visual water spots and stamp condition; screen reader compatible.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ A2 Convergence Hypothesis:** Moderate positive correlation. Handling procedural dilemmas under ambiguity directly mirrors SJT trade-off situations.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (A1, A3)
- **A2 $\leftrightarrow$ A1:** A1 tests regular sorting; A2 tests handling non-conforming items.
- **A2 $\leftrightarrow$ A3:** Both focus on detecting defects/anomalies.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure long-term thoroughness across complex multi-week projects.
