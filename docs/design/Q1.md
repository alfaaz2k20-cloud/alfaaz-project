# Mini-Game Design Sheet: Q1 — Optional Discovery
**World:** The Hidden Gallery (`W5`)  
**Target Parameter:** `curiosity`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `epistemic_exploration`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `curiosity`
- **Facet:** `epistemic_exploration`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `curiosity`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Information Seeking Without Extrinsic Reward
- **Honesty Note:** Evaluates voluntary exploration of unrequired cultural artifacts and background archives where no score incentive exists.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate navigates an exhibition floorplan to reach an exit marker. Optional side alcoves contain unrequired archival manuscripts and artifacts.
- **Trial Structure:** 1 continuous floorplan navigation with 3 optional side alcoves.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Map navigation; content is broad cultural/historical, not Alfaaz-specific.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Direct clear path to exit clearly demarcated.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `navigation_step`
- `alcove_entered`
- `artifact_examined`
- `detail_expanded`
- `exit_reached`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `optional_alcove_exploration_rate`
- **Formula:** count(alcoves entered) / total_optional_alcoves
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Propensity to voluntarily investigate non-mandatory domains.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Proportion of voluntary areas explored.

### Feature: `epistemic_dwell_duration_ratio`
- **Formula:** time_spent_in_alcoves / total_navigation_time
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Investment of attention in optional informational depth.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Relative dwell time.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `1`
- **Expected Observations:** `1`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Exited in <1000ms with zero movement trace.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Keyboard list navigation of gallery rooms and artifact descriptions.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Session terminated before navigation. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
