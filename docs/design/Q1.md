## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Research Domain / Construct:** Epistemic Curiosity, Information Seeking, & Voluntary Exploration.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Observes voluntary inspection of optional historical archival chambers when there is no extrinsic task demand or test requirement to do so.
- **Claim Boundary:** Inspecting text alcoves in a simulated courtyard does not prove lifelong intellectual curiosity or scientific inquisitiveness.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Gottlieb, J., Oudeyer, P.-Y., Lopes, M., & Baranes, A. (2013). *Information-seeking, curiosity, and attention: Computational and neural mechanisms.* Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 17, 585–593. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.001
- Kidd, C., & Hayden, B. Y. (2015). *The Psychology and Neuroscience of Curiosity.* Neuron, 88, 449–460. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2015.10.004

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Curiosity is characterized by information-seeking driven by intrinsic motivation rather than immediate instrumental reward. When presented with 3 optional side chambers detailing traditional craft techniques (pigments, Koshur paper, oral verse), curious individuals voluntarily inspect the narratives before proceeding, whereas purely task-minimalist participants bypass them immediately.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Number of optional alcoves inspected (`alcove_inspected`, count: 0 to 3).
- Dwell time spent reading uncovered archival narratives (`narrative_dwell_ms`).
- Decision to proceed immediately vs explore all chambers.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `chambers_presented` (timestamp, available_chambers: 3)
- `chamber_inspected` (timestamp, chamber_id)
- `gallery_exited` (timestamp, total_inspected, total_dwell_ms)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `voluntary_exploration_ratio`
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{count(chambers\_inspected)}}{\text{total\_optional\_chambers (3)}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates voluntary information-seeking in the absence of explicit task demands.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (3 optional chamber exploration opportunities)
- **Feature 2:** `exploration_depth_dwell_ratio`
  - *Formula:* $\min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{total\_reading\_dwell\_ms}}{\text{expected\_reading\_duration\_ms}}\right)$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Distinguishes genuine reading and information absorption from rapid superficial clicking.
  - *OBSERVATIONS_PER_FEATURE:* **3** (dwell sampled across up to 3 chambers)

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Curiosity $\neq$ Click Count:** Clicking rapidly on all 3 buttons in 0.5 seconds without reading does not indicate curiosity; dwell time and information absorption are required.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Reading Speed / Fatigue:** Fast readers may spend less time; fatigued candidates may skip to finish quickly.
- **Domain Interest:** Specific interest in calligraphy/paper vs general curiosity.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Proceed button ("Proceed Ahead &rarr;") is always visible, prominent, and active from the start, with zero penalty for immediate exit.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Narrative text formatted for standard screen readers; keyboard tab focusable buttons.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ Q1 Convergence Hypothesis:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally with SJT curiosity items (e.g. self-directed learning, exploring background context). Behavioral tasks often correlate only weakly with declarative SJT items; the magnitude of association is not pre-specified and will be estimated empirically.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (Q2, Q3)
- **Q1 $\leftrightarrow$ Q2:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Positive association expected directionally; both measure voluntary attention to unexplained or uncataloged cultural artifacts.
- **Q1 $\leftrightarrow$ Q3:** *Alfaaz design hypothesis, not a finding.* Q3 tests synthesis of discovered knowledge.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure scientific creativity or deep investigative drive across complex career domains.
