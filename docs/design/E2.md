# Mini-Game Design Sheet: E2 — Setback Recovery (The Unexpected Guest)
**World:** The Shifting Grid (`W4`)  
**Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `constructive_disruption_response`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `constructive_disruption_response`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`emotional_agility`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Coping Flexibility, Constructive Setback Management, & Context Sensitivity.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates response selection when an unexpected physical event (a wind gust tipping over a welcome easel) interrupts a setup activity.
- **Claim Boundary:** Selecting a calm response in a hypothetical vignette does not establish affective composure during severe real-world emergencies.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Bonanno, G. A., & Burton, C. L. (2013). *Regulatory Flexibility...* Perspectives on Psychological Science, 8, 591–612.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
When unexpected disruptions occur, emotionally agile volunteers do not catastrophize, freeze, or abandon ongoing obligations; they evaluate whether an immediate 2-minute fix, team coordination, or sticking to the primary schedule is most adaptive.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Selection among 3 constructive response actions (`QUICK_FIX`, `ASK_TEAM`, `FINISH_FIRST`).
- Deliberation latency.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `setback_presented` (timestamp, notice_id)
- `response_option_clicked` (timestamp, option_id)
- `response_confirmed` (timestamp, final_choice)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `adaptive_coping_selection`
  - *Formula:* Categorical mapping based on contextual priority.
  - *Units:* Score
  - *Direction:* Evaluates balance between immediate containment and long-term goal pursuit.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Emotional Agility $\neq$ Direct Internal Emotional Measurement:** Does not monitor heart rate, facial expression, or internal emotional tension.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Verbal SJT Format:** E2 in its current client implementation is effectively an 8th SJT question formatted as a mini-game.
- **Social Desirability:** All three options sound relatively reasonable and calm.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs decoy options representing rigid panic or irritable abandonment to test discriminant validity.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Textual presentation; keyboard navigable.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ E2 Convergence Hypothesis:** High correlation expected because E2 is structurally identical to an SJT item.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (E1, E3)
- Diverges from E1 because E1 is an active perceptual sorting task, whereas E2 is declarative vignette choice.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure physiological stress resilience or real-time panic under pressure.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** 1 single multiple-choice question.
- **Method Redundancy:** Replicates SJT methodology inside a game world.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on whether E2 should be redesigned into an interactive game task (e.g. dynamic timer pause and recovery) or retained as a situational prompt.
