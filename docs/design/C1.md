# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C1 — Resource Cooperation (The Artisan's Basket)
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `need_sensitive_resource_sharing`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `need_sensitive_resource_sharing`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`collaborative_spirit`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Need-Sensitive Resource Allocation, Prosocial Cooperation, & Distributive Equity.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Laboratory analog of resource distribution under asymmetric initial endowments (8 tiles vs 2 tiles).
- **Claim Boundary:** Giving away digital tiles in a simulation does not demonstrate genuine collaborative commitment or sacrifice in real team projects.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- McEwan, D., Ruissen, G. R., Eys, M. A., Zumbo, B. D., & Beauchamp, M. R. (2017). *The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training on Teamwork Behaviors and Team Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.* PLOS ONE, 12(1), e0169604. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169604
- Burke, C. S., Stagl, K. C., Salas, E., Pierce, L., & Kendall, D. (2006). *Understanding team adaptation: A conceptual analysis and model.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 1189–1207. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.91.6.1189

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Effective team members do not hoard materials necessary for collective project completion, nor do they give away tools recklessly so their own station fails. They evaluate collective needs and equalize or optimize resources so both stations succeed.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Number of ceramic tiles transferred from personal basket to partner's basket.
- Pacing of transfer adjustment (+ / - button interaction sequence).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `baskets_presented` (timestamp, user_tiles: 8, partner_tiles: 2)
- `transfer_adjusted` (timestamp, current_transfer_count)
- `resource_transfer_confirmed` (timestamp, final_shared_amount)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `resource_equity_index`
  - *Formula:* $1 - \frac{|\text{final\_user\_tiles} - \text{final\_partner\_tiles}|}{\text{initial\_difference}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Values closer to 1.0 indicate balance/cooperation, moving toward 5–5 equal distribution.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Collaborative Spirit $\neq$ Amount of Sharing:** Indiscriminately giving away all 6 tiles (leaving oneself with 2 and partner with 8) impairs one's own station. Cooperation requires mutual enablement, not self-abnegation.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Social Desirability / Compliance:** Obvious "nice" answer is giving 3 tiles to make it 5–5.
- **Arithmetic Facility:** Calculating equality (8 - 3 = 5, 2 + 3 = 5).

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs trials where partner already has sufficient resources (e.g. 5 vs 5) and sharing is unnecessary or wasteful.
- Current client lacks control trials; only presents a single 8 vs 2 trial.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Numeric increment/decrement buttons accessible via keyboard Tab/Enter.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ C1 Convergence Hypothesis:** Modest positive correlation. SJT tests team coordination dilemmas; C1 tests micro-allocation of supplies.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (C2, C3)
- **C1 $\leftrightarrow$ C2:** C1 tests material sharing; C2 tests spatial and role coordination.
- **C1 $\leftrightarrow$ C3:** C3 tests dynamic balancing under changing demands.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish interpersonal reliability, conflict resolution, or psychological safety in live teams.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Only 1 single trial in current client (`the_shared_canvas.js`).
- **Nuisance Control:** Lacks control trials where sharing is unneeded.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision specifying multi-trial schedule with non-sharing control conditions before extractor implementation.
