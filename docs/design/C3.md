# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C3 — Collaboration Repair (The Dual Lanterns)
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `collaborative_misalignment_repair`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `collaborative_misalignment_repair`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`collaborative_spirit`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Mutual Monitoring, Backup Behavior, & Teamwork Repair.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Balancing shared spotlight lanterns between two connected exhibition rooms.
- **Claim Boundary:** Moving a spotlight slider does not measure interpersonal repair following interpersonal friction or emotional conflict in real teams.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- McEwan, D., et al. (2017). *The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training...* PLOS ONE, 12(1), e0169604.
- Burke, C. S., et al. (2006). *Understanding team adaptation...* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 1189–1207.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
When team members encounter an imbalance or resource deficiency caused by shifting conditions or partner oversight, collaborative spirit manifests in proactive repair and mutual accommodation rather than blaming or ignoring the deficit.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Adjustment of spotlight slider between Room A (partner room) and Room B (candidate room).
- Convergence on balanced illumination.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `lanterns_presented` (timestamp, roomA_start, roomB_start)
- `lantern_balance_adjusted` (timestamp, roomA_val, roomB_val)
- `lantern_balance_confirmed` (timestamp, final_allocation)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `collaborative_balance_equity`
  - *Formula:* $1 - \frac{|\text{roomA} - \text{roomB}|}{\text{total\_lanterns}}$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Values closer to 1.0 indicate balanced bilateral allocation.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Collaborative Spirit $\neq$ Amount of Sharing:** Over-allocating all lanterns to the partner room leaves one's own gallery dark, sabotaging the joint exhibition.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Math Simplicity:** 5–5 split is obvious from the UI.
- **Compliance:** Choosing the exact middle without considering room dimensions.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs trials where Room A has 8 paintings and Room B has 2 paintings, requiring unequal proportional lighting rather than simple numerical parity.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Numeric display of lantern counts; keyboard slider step navigation.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ C3 Convergence Hypothesis:** Modest positive correlation.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (C1, C2)
- **C3 $\leftrightarrow$ C1:** Both measure resource distribution.
- **C3 $\leftrightarrow$ C2:** Both measure spatial and environmental coordination.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure live communicative repair between real individuals.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Only 1 trial in client.
- **Nuisance:** Trivial 5-5 default split.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on asymmetrical room demand trials and multi-step interactive repair.
