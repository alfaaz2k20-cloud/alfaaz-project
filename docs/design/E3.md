# Mini-Game Design Sheet: E3 — Changing Conditions / Aesthetic Equilibrium (The Geometric Harmony)

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `emotional_agility`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `equilibrium_under_ambiguity`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`emotional_agility`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Ambiguity Tolerance & Strategy Revision Under Changing Aesthetics.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Selecting a balancing decorative motif when exhibition hall aesthetic constraints vary.
- **Claim Boundary:** Selecting a geometric pattern does not measure psychological resilience or emotional balance in challenging life circumstances.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Bonanno, G. A., & Burton, C. L. (2013). *Regulatory Flexibility...* Perspectives on Psychological Science, 8, 591–612.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Individuals who adapt effectively maintain composure and equilibrium when instructions are open-ended or aesthetic parameters conflict, finding a harmonious balance rather than becoming paralyzed by lack of explicit direction.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Selection among 3 motif patterns (`T1: Diamond`, `T2: Wave`, `T3: Dot Grid`).
- Latency and option inspection prior to choice.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `motifs_presented` (timestamp, available_options)
- `motif_card_clicked` (timestamp, motif_id)
- `motif_confirmed` (timestamp, final_motif)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `ambiguity_balance_preference`
  - *Formula:* Categorical mapping.
  - *Units:* Score
  - *Direction:* Evaluates aesthetic balance.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Emotional Agility $\neq$ Direct Internal Emotional Measurement:** Does not assess internal mood or feelings.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Subjective Taste:** Pure aesthetic preference (liking curves vs dots) rather than psychological agility.
- **Cognitive Load:** Minimal.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Requires presenting discordant background styles and evaluating whether choice adapts to the background or reflects rigid personal preference.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Textual descriptions of motif styling alongside visual icons.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ E3 Convergence Hypothesis:** Low correlation expected due to aesthetic preference confound.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (E1, E2)
- Low convergence expected under current implementation.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish emotional endurance or affective regulation.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** 1 single choice among 3 cards.
- **Severe Confound:** Confounded by personal aesthetic taste.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision to establish a valid behavioral adaptation mechanic rather than subjective pattern preference.
