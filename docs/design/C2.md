# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C2 — Coordination
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `interpersonal_synchrony_and_pacing`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Facet:** `interpersonal_synchrony_and_pacing`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `collaborative_spirit`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Adaptive Interpersonal Pacing
- **Honesty Note:** Measures behavioral pacing adjustment when working on interconnected canvas sections.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Player and partner must alternate or complement brush strokes to blend boundary gradients without overwriting each other's work.
- **Trial Structure:** 3 segments where partner's pace dynamically modulates (steady, delayed, accelerated).

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Rhythmic timing; focuses on collision avoidance and complementary pacing rather than motor dexterity.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Independent canvas zones where partner pace is irrelevant.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `brush_stroke_start`
- `partner_stroke_detected`
- `overlap_collision_event`
- `pacing_delay_adjusted`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `coordination_collision_avoidance_rate`
- **Formula:** 1 - (count(destructive overlaps) / total_joint_strokes)
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Higher rate indicates respectful spatial-temporal awareness of peer actions.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Ratio of non-conflicting joint actions.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `2`
- **Expected Observations:** `3`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `30000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Zero strokes or intentional continuous overwrite.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Auditory/visual pacing metronome indicators; step-based turn mode.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Less than 2 segments attempted. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
