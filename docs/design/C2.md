# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C2 — Spatial / Role Coordination (The Gallery Wall)
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `complementary_role_coordination`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `complementary_role_coordination`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`collaborative_spirit`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Mutual Adaptation, Role Complementarity, & Spatial Task Coordination.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Selecting an artwork placement slot that aesthetically balances with a colleague's hung piece.
- **Claim Boundary:** Selecting a balanced visual slot in a diagram does not prove complex team coordination in multifaceted projects.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- McEwan, D., et al. (2017). *The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training...* PLOS ONE, 12(1), e0169604.
- Burke, C. S., et al. (2006). *Understanding team adaptation: A conceptual analysis and model.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 91, 1189–1207.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Collaborative work requires coordinating one's actions with what a teammate has already built. When hanging art, placing a piece in an evenly balanced slot respects the teammate's contribution and achieves collective harmony.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Slot selection among 3 options (`SLOT_TOP`, `SLOT_RIGHT`, `SLOT_BOTTOM`).
- Pacing and inspection of options before confirmation.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `wall_presented` (timestamp, partner_position: "left")
- `wall_slot_selected` (timestamp, slot_id)
- `wall_coordination_complete` (timestamp, final_slot)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `coordination_balance_score`
  - *Formula:* Categorical mapping (`SLOT_RIGHT` [Even Spacing] = 1.0, `SLOT_TOP` = 0.5, `SLOT_BOTTOM` = 0.0)
  - *Units:* Score (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher indicates selection of complementary arrangement.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Collaborative Spirit $\neq$ Passive Deference:** Coordination means active, balanced contribution, not crowding the teammate or disappearing into a corner.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Spatial / Aesthetic Ability:** Visual perception of symmetry rather than social coordination.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs trials where partner placement is off-center or asymmetrical, requiring dynamic compensation.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Textual descriptions of spatial slots ("Right Center Slot - Even Spacing") alongside visual canvas.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ C2 Convergence Hypothesis:** Low-to-moderate positive correlation.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (C1, C3)
- **C2 $\leftrightarrow$ C1:** Complementary: material sharing vs spatial coordination.
- **C2 $\leftrightarrow$ C3:** Both address spatial and functional balance.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot establish spontaneous collaborative negotiation or verbal compromise.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Currently implemented as a single 1-click slot selection.
- **Nuisance:** Strongly loaded on visual symmetry perception rather than social cooperation.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on whether C2 requires a dynamic multi-step placement flow or interactive turn-taking.
