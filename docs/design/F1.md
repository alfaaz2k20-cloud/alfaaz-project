## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `empathy`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `attunement_to_cues`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`empathy`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Emotion/State Cue Sensitivity & Affective/Cognitive Perspective-Taking.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Laboratory analog of real-time social cue detection under cognitive load. Observes behavioral response latency and directional adjustment when partner cues appear during an acoustic tuning activity.
- **Claim Boundary:** Task-specific behavioral responsiveness does not establish an overall empathy trait, moral compassion, or clinical socio-emotional capacity.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Healey, M. L., & Grossman, M. (2018). *Cognitive and Affective Perspective-Taking: Evidence for Shared and Dissociable Anatomical Substrates.* Frontiers in Neurology, 9, 491. https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2018.00491
- Christian, M. S., Edwards, B. D., & Bradley, J. C. (2010). *Situational Judgment Tests: Constructs Assessed and a Meta-Analysis of Their Criterion-Related Validities.* Personnel Psychology, 63, 83–117.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
When working alongside colleagues, empathetic collaboration requires detecting subtle changes in partner comfort or requirements and adjusting one's operational settings accordingly. The task presents an audio-frequency dial where a simulated partner gives feedback on acoustic comfort, requiring the participant to notice the cue and adjust the slider toward the comfort zone.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Time elapsed from partner cue display to initial slider adjustment (`cue_response_latency_ms`).
- Final slider position relative to the partner's target acoustic preference (`contextual_adjustment_accuracy`).

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `partner_cue_onset` (timestamp, cue type: comfort vs distraction)
- `slider_input` (timestamp, value: 0–100)
- `tuning_locked` (timestamp, final_value, latency_ms, accuracy)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `cue_response_latency_ms`
  - *Formula:* $t(\text{first slider movement after cue}) - t(\text{cue onset})$
  - *Units:* Milliseconds (ms)
  - *Direction:* Lower latency indicates faster attunement to partner state change.
- **Feature 2:** `contextual_adjustment_accuracy`
  - *Formula:* $\max(0, 1 - \frac{|\text{final\_value} - \text{partner\_target}|}{\text{tolerance\_range}})$
  - *Units:* Ratio (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher ratio indicates precise accommodation to the partner's acoustic comfort.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Empathy $\neq$ Indiscriminate Accommodation:** Empathetic behavior is context-sensitive attunement, not automatic submission to every cue or continuous over-adjustment.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Motor Latency / Precision:** High slider friction or touchpad differences affect response latency.
- **Visual Contrast & Pacing:** Waveform animation may distract from partner dialogue text.
- **Alternative Explanation:** Candidate moved slider toward 35 purely by visual symmetry or trial-and-error hunting rather than reading partner cue.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Required Control:** Neutral drift trial where partner states comfort is optimal and frequency drift is purely mechanical. In this control trial, adjusting for the partner is unnecessary.
- **Current Client Defect:** Client implementation currently has only 1 single trial and lacks the neutral control trial.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- Slider must support keyboard arrows (Step size: 5).
- Latency metrics must be excluded or flagged as unadjusted in assistive keyboard/screen-reader modes.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ F1 Convergence Hypothesis:** Modest positive correlation. Both reflect attentiveness to interpersonal needs, but SJT tests explicit policy judgment in complex vignettes, whereas F1 tests real-time reactive perceptual cue response. Divergence reflects method variance (declarative judgment vs live reaction).

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (F2, F3)
- **F1 $\leftrightarrow$ F2:** F1 tests immediate cue latency; F2 tests handling ambiguity when cues are unclear. Low-to-moderate positive association expected.
- **F1 $\leftrightarrow$ F3:** F3 tests updating behavior when context changes.
- **Internal Structure:** Three tasks capture complementary facets (latency, disambiguation, context updating) to triangulate interpersonal responsiveness without relying on a single mechanic.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot diagnose clinical empathy, compassion, or deep emotional care.
- Cannot establish interpersonal functioning across long-term relationships.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Opportunities:** Currently only 1 trial in client (`frontend/src/recruit_games/the_frequency.js`). 1 observation is psychometrically inadequate.
- **Nuisance Control:** Lacks non-accommodation decoy trial.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision specifying a multi-trial schedule (minimum 4 trials) and non-accommodation control trials before extractor implementation.
