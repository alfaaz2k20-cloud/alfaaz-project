# Mini-Game Design Sheet: Q3 — Information Integration (The Weaver's Chronicle)
**World:** The Hidden Gallery (`W5`)  
**Target Parameter:** `curiosity`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `downstream_information_application`  
**Measurement Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Target Parameter:** `curiosity`
- **Behavioral Facet:** `downstream_information_application`
- **Authoritative Definition Reference:** Bound to `config/parameters.json` (`curiosity`).

## 2. Nearest Established Research Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Downstream Retention and Practical Application of Explored Information.
- **Honesty Match Note:** Evaluates whether cultural knowledge discovered during optional exploration in Q1 is retained and applied when organizing chronicle records.
- **Claim Boundary:** Answering a recall question about previously read text tests verbal working memory more than epistemic motivation.

## 3. Research Citation(s)
- Gottlieb, J., et al. (2013). *Information-seeking, curiosity, and attention...* Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 17, 585–593.
- Kidd, C., & Hayden, B. Y. (2015). *The Psychology and Neuroscience of Curiosity.* Neuron, 88, 449–460.

## 4. Mechanistic Rationale
Curiosity is not merely collecting random impressions; curious individuals absorb and synthesize what they discover, enabling them to connect past observations to subsequent challenges.

## 5. Exact Observable Behavior
- Answering chronicle classification or recall questions referencing details from Q1/Q2.

## 6. Candidate Raw Telemetry Requirements
- `chronicle_question_presented` (timestamp, item_id)
- `option_selected` (timestamp, choice, is_correct)

## 7. Candidate Feature(s), Formula, and Direction
- **Feature 1:** `integrated_knowledge_application`
  - *Formula:* Binary/Ratio accuracy on questions drawing on optional Q1 narrative details.
  - *Units:* Accuracy (0.0 to 1.0)
  - *Direction:* Higher score indicates effective synthesis of previously explored information.

## 8. Important Construct Boundary
- **Curiosity $\neq$ Verbal Memory Testing:** The assessment must not degenerate into a reading comprehension memory quiz.

## 9. Nuisance Demands & Alternative Explanations
- **Working Memory Confound:** If candidates explored Q1 but forgot the exact technical term, they score low despite high curiosity.
- **Incidental Reading Ability:** High verbal intelligence vs genuine curiosity.

## 10. Required Control / Decoy Conditions
- Needs questions where the answer can be deduced through logical connection rather than verbatim rote recall.

## 11. Accessibility Implications
- High-contrast text; keyboard selection.

## 12. Expected Relationship with SJT
- **SJT $\leftrightarrow$ Q3 Convergence Hypothesis:** Low-to-moderate positive correlation.

## 13. Expected Relationship with Sibling Mini-Games (Q1, Q2)
- Depends heavily on whether candidate visited Q1 chambers.

## 14. What the Task Cannot Establish
- Cannot measure curiosity independently of working memory capacity under current design.

## 15. Pre-Implementation Validity Check & Decision
- **Severe Confound:** In current client, Q3 functions as a short-term memory test on Q1 text.
- **Status:** `MISSING DESIGN DECISION — STOPPED`.
- **Blocker:** Requires owner design decision on decoupling curiosity application from rote working-memory recall before implementation.
