# ALFAAZ COLLECTIVE
## PRIVACY, DATA PROTECTION & CANDIDATE ASSESSMENT CONFIDENTIALITY CONSENT
### Schedule C — Data Protection Accord & Non-Disclosure Consent

> **STATUTORY COMPLIANCE DECLARATION:**  
> This Consent and Non-Disclosure Instrument is executed in strict compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, 2023)**, the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, and the *Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011*. It establishes the terms governing the collection, processing, and safeguarding of volunteer personal data, as well as binding obligations protecting the proprietary integrity of the Alfaaz Talent Assessment Engine (V2.1).

---

### PART I: PROPRIETARY INTEGRITY OF THE ALFAAZ TALENT ASSESSMENT (V2.1)

#### 1. RECOGNITION OF PROPRIETARY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
1.1. The Volunteer acknowledges that Alfaaz Collective has designed and developed a specialized, scientifically calibrated recruitment battery known as the **Alfaaz Talent Assessment System (Version 2.1)**, comprising:
- Multi-dimensional Situational Judgment Tests (SJTs) grounded in cultural integrity, curatorial judgment, and public engagement;
- Theoretical calibration rubrics, psychometric trait weightings, and psychometric evaluation matrices;
- Recruiter evaluation dossiers, research instrumentation (including `research.html` protocols), and structured interview frameworks;
- Scoring algorithms and candidate fitment benchmarks.

1.2. The Volunteer recognizes that all the aforesaid materials, methodologies, questions, scenarios, and scoring rubrics constitute valuable, proprietary intellectual property, trade secrets, and confidential assets of Alfaaz Collective.

#### 2. VOLUNTEER NON-DISCLOSURE COVENANTS
2.1. In consideration of having participated in the assessment and being welcomed into the Collective, the Volunteer unequivocally agrees that they shall **NOT** at any time:
- Copy, reproduce, screenshot, photograph, audio/video record, transcribe, or digitally download any test questions, scenario prompts, or assessment interfaces;
- Disclose, publish, distribute, or leak test questions, ideal responses, scoring mechanics, or evaluation rubrics to future applicants, educational coaching groups, social media platforms, or online forums;
- Create derivative test questions or coaching materials based upon Alfaaz’s assessment battery;
- Assist, coach, or improperly advise prospective candidates in taking or bypassing the assessment battery.

2.2. **Protection of Fellow Applicant Data:** If the Volunteer is entrusted with or inadvertently comes into contact with candidate rosters, applicant responses, test scores, resumes, or interview notes:
- The Volunteer shall hold all such applicant Personal Identifiable Information (PII) in strict confidence;
- Never share applicant details with outside parties;
- Never use candidate data for personal networking, commercial solicitation, or unauthorized communication.

### PART II: VOLUNTEER PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING CONSENT (DPDP ACT, 2023)

#### 3. PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED BY THE COLLECTIVE
To facilitate an organized, safe, and accountable volunteer engagement, Alfaaz Collective collects and processes the following necessary categories of personal data:
- **Identification & Demographic Data:** Full legal name, date of birth, gender, photograph, and government-issued identity proof (Aadhaar number, Voter ID, or Passport) for age and identity verification;
- **Contact Details:** Permanent and current residential address in Jammu & Kashmir or elsewhere, primary telephone/WhatsApp number, and personal email address;
- **Emergency Data:** Name, relationship, and contact telephone number of designated emergency contacts;
- **Financial Details (For Honorarium & Reimbursements):** Bank account number, IFSC code, or Unified Payments Interface (UPI) handle, collected solely for disbursing approved out-of-pocket reimbursements and discretionary stipends;
- **Media & Archival Footage:** Photographs, audio, or video recordings taken during public exhibitions, gallery sessions, workshops, and official group documentation.

#### 4. SPECIFIED AND LAWFUL PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
The Volunteer explicitly consents to Alfaaz Collective collecting, storing, and processing their personal data strictly for the following lawful purposes:
- **Operational Coordination:** Scheduling exhibition rosters, gallery floor coordination, volunteer communication, and task assignment;
- **Health & Safety:** Ensuring physical security at venues, crowd management, and contacting emergency next-of-kin in the event of an accident or medical need;
- **Accounting & Verification:** Processing verified expense reimbursements, disbursing honorarium amounts, and fulfilling statutory accounting records;
- **Cultural Archiving & Public Credit:** Crediting the Volunteer in exhibition brochures, printed catalogs, the official website, and archival dispatches (including *Tchandervar*) as a valued cultural contributor.

#### 5. DATA SAFEGUARDS, STORAGE & CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1. **Security Standards:** Alfaaz Collective implements reasonable security practices, technical access restrictions, and administrative safeguards to protect volunteer personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

5.2. **No Commercial Sale or Renting:** Alfaaz Collective shall **NEVER** sell, rent, commercialize, or share volunteer personal data with third-party marketing firms, commercial advertisers, or unrelated agencies.

5.3. **Limited Third-Party Disclosures:** Personal data may only be shared with:
- Authorized banking partners and payment gateways solely for reimbursement/honorarium transfers;
- Government or law enforcement authorities only when mandated by applicable statutory law or judicial summons;
- Partner heritage venues (e.g., Mahatta Art Gallery, Nigeen Club) strictly for security entry clearances.

#### 6. DATA RETENTION & VOLUNTEER RIGHTS
6.1. **Retention Period:** Personal data shall be retained only for the duration of the voluntary engagement and for a reasonable statutory period thereafter (up to 3 years for financial expense records and audit verification).

6.2. **Volunteer Rights under DPDP Act, 2023:**
- **Right to Access & Review:** The Volunteer may request a summary of personal data held by Alfaaz;
- **Right to Correction & Updating:** The Volunteer has the right to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Right to Erasure:** Upon completion of voluntary service, the Volunteer may request the deletion of non-statutory personal data;
- **Right to Nominate:** The Volunteer may nominate an individual to act on their behalf regarding personal data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6.3. **Grievance Redressal:** Any inquiry, request, or grievance concerning personal data may be lodged with the Curatorial Data Desk via email at:  
**`alfaazcollective@gmail.com`** (Subject: *Attention: Data Privacy Officer*).

### PART III: ARTIST & PATRON DATA CONFIDENTIALITY

#### 7. SACROSANCT STATUS OF ARTIST & PATRON RECORDS
7.1. **Artist Privacy:** The Volunteer acknowledges that the personal phone numbers, studio addresses, unlisted contact details, private pricing arrangements, and personal life histories of participating artists are privileged and confidential. The Volunteer shall not disclose or broadcast such details under any circumstances.

7.2. **Patron & Collector Lists:** Visitor guestbooks, collector contact registers, art acquisition records, and donor listings maintained at exhibition desks are strictly confidential. Volunteers shall never copy, screenshot, export, or share visitor logs with any external party.
