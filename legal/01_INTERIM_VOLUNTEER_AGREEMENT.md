### 1. PREAMBLE & INSTITUTIONAL CONTEXT

1.1. **The Collective:** Alfaaz Collective is an independent cultural initiative established in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir in 2020. The Collective acts as an artistic sanctuary and contemplative bridge connecting painting, poetry, literature, cinema, and lived philosophy through curated physical exhibitions (including *Kaamil*, *Bayaan*, *Khayaal*, *Harud*, *The Swan*, and live painting ateliers), literary circles, print publications, and digital archives (including sister offshoot *Tchandervar*).

1.2. **NGO-in-Formation Status:** The Collective is currently undergoing formal statutory registration under the applicable Societies Registration / Public Trusts enactments in Jammu & Kashmir. Until the issuance of the formal registration certificate by the competent authority, the Collective operates legally as an Unincorporated Voluntary Association administered by its founding curators and management committee.

1.3. **Voluntary Intent:** The Volunteer, having demonstrated cultural alignment, ethical diligence, and shared passion through the Alfaaz Talent Assessment (V2.1 Battery) and curatorial dialogue, desires to contribute their skills, time, and enthusiasm to the mission of the Collective in a purely voluntary and honorary capacity.

---

### 2. LEGAL NATURE OF ENGAGEMENT (STRICT NON-EMPLOYMENT COVENANT)

2.1. **No Employment Relationship:** This Agreement is an agreement for voluntary service and **DOES NOT** constitute a contract of employment, appointment letter, apprenticeship, partnership, joint venture, or master-servant relationship under the *Industrial Disputes Act, 1947*, the *Code on Wages, 2019*, the *Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952*, the *Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948*, or any other state or central labor enactment.

2.2. **No Claim to Remuneration or Permanent Role:** The Volunteer acknowledges and affirms that:
- Their contribution is motivated exclusively by civic, cultural, and personal devotion to arts and letters;
- They are not entitled to wages, salary, overtime pay, statutory bonus, gratuity, severance compensation, or paid leave;
- Nothing in this Agreement guarantees or implies any future regular, permanent, or paid employment with Alfaaz Collective upon its formal statutory incorporation.

2.3. **Independent Status:** The Volunteer retains complete autonomy over their personal, academic, or professional pursuits outside the agreed volunteer commitments.

---

### 3. SCOPE OF VOLUNTEER ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1. **Core Domains of Contribution:** Depending on mutual curatorial alignment, the Volunteer may assist in:
- **Exhibition Coordination:** Assisting with setup, installation, gallery ushering, artist hospitality, and visitor engagement at partner venues across Srinagar (e.g., Mahatta Art Gallery, The Bund, Nigeen Club, The Swan);
- **Archival & Literary Documentation:** Assisting in cataloging artworks, transcribing poetry, archiving exhibition photography, and contributing editorial texts;
- **Community & Dialogue Sessions:** Facilitating reading clubs, discussion circles, and visitor guidance;
- **Digital & Publication Outreaches:** Supporting curation for web documentation, newsletter publication, and *Tchandervar* archival dispatches.

3.2. **Time Commitment:** The Volunteer agrees to contribute on a flexible, mutually scheduled basis (typically 4 to 8 hours per week, with focused availability during live exhibition weekends), coordinated respectfully through official communication channels.

3.3. **Supervision & Mentorship:** The Volunteer will coordinate their activities under the guidance of the Lead Curator or designated Venue Manager.

---

### 4. EXPENSES, STIPEND & HONORARIUM (EX-GRATIA)

4.1. **Out-of-Pocket Reimbursements:** The Collective shall reimburse verified, pre-approved direct expenses incurred by the Volunteer exclusively in the performance of designated tasks (such as local travel within Srinagar between exhibition venues, printing supplies, or curatorial materials), provided that:
- The expenditure was authorized in writing or via official message by an Authorized Curator prior to being incurred;
- Valid physical or digital tax invoices/receipts are submitted to the administration.

4.2. **Ex-Gratia Honorarium / Fellowship Allowance (If Applicable):** In instances where the Collective awards a discretionary monthly honorarium, event stipend, or conveyance fellowship:
- Such amount is strictly an **ex-gratia, honorary contribution** intended to defray personal conveyance, meals, and incidental volunteer costs;
- It shall not be construed as taxable salary, wage, or commercial consideration for services rendered;
- The disbursement, frequency, and amount of any honorarium remain entirely at the sole curatorial discretion of the Collective and subject to available cultural patronage funds.

---

### 5. ARTIST INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & MORAL RIGHTS SAFEGUARDING

5.1. **Sanctity of Artist Works (Indian Copyright Act, 1957):** The Volunteer expressly recognizes that all physical and digital artworks—including oil paintings, watercolors, photographic plates, calligraphy scrolls, sculptures, architectural sketches, poetic manuscripts, and essays—exhibited or archived by Alfaaz Collective are the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the respective originating artists or their legal heirs under the *Copyright Act, 1957*.

5.2. **Moral Rights:** The moral rights of contributing artists guaranteed under Section 57 of the *Copyright Act, 1957* (right of paternity and right against distortion or mutilation) are inviolable. The Volunteer shall not:
- Disparage, alter, deface, or modify any artwork or literary text;
- Remove, obscure, or crop any artist signature, colophon, or watermarked attribution;
- Photograph or record artworks for unauthorized commercial reproduction, prints, NFTs, merchandise, or third-party syndication.

5.3. **Works Created for the Collective:** Any original promotional material, social media graphics, event photographs, or curatorial dispatches created by the Volunteer specifically on behalf of Alfaaz Collective during their engagement shall be deemed licensed to Alfaaz Collective on a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive worldwide basis for archival, non-commercial, and cultural promotional use. The Collective shall credit the Volunteer as contributor wherever practicable.

---

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY & PROPRIETARY INTEGRITY

6.1. **Protected Information:** The Volunteer agrees to maintain strict, unbroken confidentiality regarding:
- Unpublished exhibition designs, curatorial drafts, theme concepts, and unannounced artist line-ups;
- Personal contact details, home addresses, phone numbers, and private correspondence of participating artists, patrons, and collectors;
- The proprietary methodology, scoring matrices, situational judgment scenarios, and psychometric rubrics of the **Alfaaz Talent Assessment System (V2.1)** and candidate dossiers;
- Financial patronage, donor ledgers, and operational deliberations of the Curatorial Committee.

6.2. **Perpetual Obligation:** The obligation of confidentiality shall survive the expiration, pause, or termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.

---

### 7. CODE OF CONDUCT & COMMUNITY STANDARDS

7.1. **Incorporation by Reference:** The Volunteer affirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to be strictly bound by the **Alfaaz Volunteer Code of Conduct & Heritage Venue Etiquette** (Schedule B), which forms an integral part of this Agreement.

7.2. **Core Standards:** The Volunteer covenants to:
- Maintain complete professionalism, warmth, and artistic reverence in all public interactions;
- Uphold zero-tolerance for any form of harassment, discrimination, verbal abuse, or substance intoxication at any Collective venue or program;
- Handle physical artworks with extreme care and adhere strictly to white-glove protocols and security standards.

---

### 8. HEALTH, SAFETY & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1. **Assumption of Voluntary Participation:** The Volunteer participates in Alfaaz Collective’s programs voluntarily and at their own initiative. While the Collective implements reasonable precautions to ensure safe, hospitable gallery and exhibition environments, the Collective shall not be held liable for any accidental loss, personal property damage, or injury sustained during transit to or participation in community events, save where caused by proven gross negligence of the Collective.

8.2. **Medical Disclosure:** The Volunteer shall inform the Collective in writing of any severe medical conditions, allergies, or physical constraints that may affect their safety during installation work or outdoor events.

---

### 9. TERM, PAUSE & TERMINATION

9.1. **Term:** This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and shall remain valid for an interim term of six (6) months, or until superseding formal membership/fellowship agreements are executed following statutory NGO registration.

9.2. **Voluntary Withdrawal (By Volunteer):** The Volunteer may resign or pause their engagement at any time by tendering seven (7) days' prior written notice (via email or official messaging) to the Lead Curator, ensuring an orderly handover of any assigned tasks.

9.3. **Curatorial Discontinuation (By Collective):** The Collective may immediately suspend or terminate this voluntary engagement without prior notice in the event of:
- Any material breach of the Code of Conduct, harassment policy, or confidentiality covenants;
- Negligent handling or endangerment of original artworks;
- Unprofessional conduct that damages the cultural reputation or communal goodwill of Alfaaz Collective or its partners.

9.4. **Handover of Property:** Upon termination or resignation, the Volunteer shall promptly return all Collective keys, badges, physical exhibition materials, inventory records, and equipment in their custody.

---

### 10. GOVERNING LAW, DISPUTE RESOLUTION & JURISDICTION

10.1. **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and governed in accordance with the substantive laws of the Republic of India.

10.2. **Curatorial Conciliation:** Any misunderstanding, grievance, or dispute arising out of or in connection with this voluntary engagement shall first be referred to an informal, good-faith conciliation process conducted by the Curatorial Advisory Council of Alfaaz Collective.

10.3. **Exclusive Jurisdiction:** In the event that conciliation fails, the competent courts situated in **Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir** shall have sole and exclusive jurisdiction to adjudicate any claims or disputes arising under this Agreement.
