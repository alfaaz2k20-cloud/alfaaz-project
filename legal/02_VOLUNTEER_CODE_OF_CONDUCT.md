# ALFAAZ COLLECTIVE
## VOLUNTEER CODE OF CONDUCT & HERITAGE VENUE ETIQUETTE
### Schedule B — Operational Standards & Curatorial Decorum

> **"A bridge between art, literature, and existence."**  
> Alfaaz Collective was founded in Srinagar in 2020 to cultivate a dignified sanctuary where human thought, poetry, painting, and visual memory meet. Every volunteer who wears our badge is a custodian of this sanctuary. This Code of Conduct outlines the ethical, behavioral, and physical standards expected of all participants.

---

### 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY & AMBASSADORSHIP

1.1. **The Spirit of Humility & Reverence:** We approach our cultural work not as mere event organizers, but as quiet custodians of memory. Volunteers are expected to conduct themselves with warmth, patience, intellectual humility, and profound respect for artists, guests, and community elders.

1.2. **Representation:** During all exhibitions, literary circles, screenings, and outreach events, volunteers serve as the living face of Alfaaz Collective. Your speech, demeanor, and attentiveness must reflect the dignity and aesthetic grace of our mission.

---

### 2. HERITAGE VENUE ETIQUETTE (SRINAGAR & PARTNER SPACES)

Alfaaz Collective hosts programs across historic, architectural, and ecologically delicate venues in Kashmir. Each venue demands particular care:

2.1. **Mahatta Art Gallery & The Bund (Srinagar):**
- **Historical Sanctity:** Mahatta Art Gallery, operating along the historic Jhelum Bund since 1915, holds irreplaceable photographic archives and vintage darkroom heritage.
- **Physical Caution:** The wooden floors, heritage glass cabinets, and vintage cameras must be treated with utmost gentleness. Avoid running, heavy stamping, or leaning against heritage woodwork or display vitrines.
- **Riverfront Decorum:** Maintain peaceful, contemplative decorum along The Bund. Ensure walkways and public river access remain unblocked by exhibition gear or crowds.

2.2. **Nigeen Club & Lakeside Venues:**
- **Ecological Responsibility:** Zero tolerance for litter, plastic waste, cigarette butts, or debris. The lake environs must be left cleaner than we found them.
- **Noise Sensitivity:** Sound levels must remain gentle. Musical or poetry amplification must be kept within curatorial thresholds so as not to disturb the lake’s natural calm or residential neighborhoods.

2.3. **The Swan & Studio Ateliers:**
- **Artisan Spaces:** When working within artist studios, workshops, or independent cultural spaces, volunteers must never displace tools, pigments, or ongoing works without explicit artist instruction.

2.4. **Neighborhood & Cultural Respect:**
- Respect local Kashmiri customs, prayer hours, and community peace.
- Maintain a modest, respectful, and professional presence at all times.

---

### 3. CURATORIAL ART HANDLING PROTOCOLS (ZERO COMPROMISE)

Original artworks are irreplaceable cultural assets. The following handling rules are mandatory:

3.1. **Glove Policy for Fine Art & Manuscripts:**
- Clean, lint-free white cotton gloves must be worn whenever handling unvarnished oil paintings, raw canvas, historical silver gelatin prints, or rare calligraphy manuscripts.
- Never touch the face or painted surface of any artwork with bare hands or fingers; natural skin oils, moisture, and perspiration cause irreversible chemical degradation.

3.2. **Handling Custom-Framed Walnut Wood Works:**
- Many Alfaaz pieces feature hand-carved Kashmiri walnut wood framing. These frames are dense, heavy, and delicate.
- **Two-Hand Rule:** Always carry framed artworks using both hands, gripping firmly from the structural side rails of the frame.
- **Never Lift by Hanging Wire or Top Rail Alone:** Never carry a work by its hanging cord, wire, screw eyes, or the top molding alone, as the joint joints may shear under weight.
- **Glass Safety:** Never apply pressure to the glass glazing or acrylic face while carrying.

3.3. **The Two-Meter Buffer Zone (Food & Liquid Prohibition):**
- **Strict Prohibition:** Absolutely no cups of Nun Chai (salt tea), Kehwa, coffee, water glasses, soft drinks, or open plates of food are permitted within two (2) meters of any displayed or stacked artwork.
- All eating and drinking must take place in designated volunteer pantry or hospitality zones.

3.4. **Stacking & Transit Rules:**
- If artworks must be temporarily placed on the floor during installation, always place them face-to-face or back-to-back with acid-free foam/cardboard interleaving pads. Never rest an artwork directly onto bare stone, concrete, or wet floors without clean runners or padding beneath.
- Report any visible scratch, frame looseness, canvas dent, or water ingress immediately to the Lead Curator.

---

### 4. WORKPLACE SAFETY & ANTI-HARASSMENT (POSH ZERO-TOLERANCE)

4.1. **Dignified Environment:** Alfaaz Collective is committed to maintaining a workplace and creative community free from harassment, intimidation, prejudice, and exploitation.

4.2. **Absolute Zero-Tolerance on Sexual Harassment:**
- In alignment with the principles of the *Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH)*, the Collective strictly prohibits any unwelcome physical contact, sexual advances, sexually colored remarks, inappropriate staring, non-consensual photography, or sending suggestive messages.
- Any violation will result in immediate termination of the volunteer engagement and referral to competent legal authorities.

4.3. **Equality & Inclusivity:**
- Every artist, visitor, and fellow volunteer must be treated with equal dignity, regardless of gender, faith, caste, background, or physical ability.
- Verbal abuse, derogatory slurs, sectarian strife, or hostile behavior will result in instant expulsion.

---

### 5. SUBSTANCE POLICY (STRICT PROHIBITION)

5.1. **Zero Substance Use:**
- Consuming alcohol, narcotics, recreational drugs, or being under the influence of any intoxicating substance while representing Alfaaz Collective, inside exhibition halls, or during program hours is strictly forbidden.
- Smoking or vaping inside exhibition halls or near heritage woodwork is an acute fire hazard and strictly banned.

---

### 6. PUBLIC STATEMENTS, MEDIA & DIGITAL ENGAGEMENT

6.1. **Official Spokesperson Protocol:**
- Only the Founding Curators or designated Management Committee members are authorized to issue official statements, speak to journalists, or give media interviews on behalf of Alfaaz Collective.
- If approached by reporters, news portals, or television cameras during an exhibition, volunteers must politely direct them to the Lead Curator or Media Desk.

6.2. **Personal Social Media Guidelines:**
- **Encouraged:** Sharing your authentic reflections, posting high-quality approved exhibition photographs, celebrating artists, and tagging `@alfaaz.2020` or `#AlfaazCollective`.
- **Prohibited:**
  - Posting candid or backstage photos of artists without their explicit verbal or written permission;
  - Leaking unreleased art, unpublished poetry, or unannounced exhibition dates;
  - Engaging in online polemics, hostile arguments, or controversial political claims using the name or branding of Alfaaz Collective.

---

### 7. EMERGENCIES & GRIEVANCE ESCALATION

7.1. **Medical or Fire Emergencies:**
- Immediately inform the Venue Lead and ensure visitor safety through marked fire exits.
- First aid kits are maintained at the reception/curatorial desk at all exhibition venues.

7.2. **Grievance Redressal:**
- If you experience or witness any form of mistreatment, harassment, or ethical compromise, you are urged to report it immediately to the Curatorial Grievance Council:
  - **Confidential Email:** `alfaazcollective@gmail.com`
  - **Direct WhatsApp Desk:** `+91 70066 13891`
- All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality, impartiality, and swift protective action.
