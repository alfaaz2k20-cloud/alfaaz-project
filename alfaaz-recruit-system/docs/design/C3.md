# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C3 — Collaboration Repair
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `constructive_alignment_repair`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Facet:** `constructive_alignment_repair`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `collaborative_spirit`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Collaborative Misalignment Repair
- **Honesty Note:** Assesses willingness to constructively realign and rectify shared discrepancies without assigning blame or abandoning joint output.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** A simulated partner inadvertently places mismatched color tiles in the shared motif. Candidate can repair, blend, redistribute, or isolate.
- **Trial Structure:** 3 repair scenarios (minor misalignment, major color clash, intentional artistic divergence).

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Spatial composition judgment; differentiated from C1/C2 by focusing on resolution of partner errors.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Scenario where partner divergence was an intended stylistic feature defined in the motif brief.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `misalignment_revealed`
- `repair_strategy_selected`
- `tile_rearranged`
- `motif_completed`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `constructive_repair_score`
- **Formula:** weighted_sum(integrative_solutions) / total_repair_trials
- **Units:** score (0-1)
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Higher score reflects constructive harmonizing of collective output.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Evaluates structural quality of repair strategy.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `2`
- **Expected Observations:** `3`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** No interaction during repair phase.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Keyboard selectable repair actions (1: Re-tile, 2: Harmonize border, 3: Retain).

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Less than 2 scenarios completed. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
