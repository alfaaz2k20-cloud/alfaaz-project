# Mini-Game Design Sheet: C1 — Resource Cooperation
**World:** The Shared Canvas (`W3`)  
**Target Parameter:** `collaborative_spirit`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `prosocial_resource_sharing`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `collaborative_spirit`
- **Facet:** `prosocial_resource_sharing`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `collaborative_spirit`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Prosocial Resource Allocation in Joint Tasks
- **Honesty Note:** Evaluates voluntary allocation of finite creative tiles/pigments when partner indicates scarcity.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Player and a simulated peer collaborate to restore a mosaic mural. Player holds surplus pigment blocks; partner's palette runs low on a shared section.
- **Trial Structure:** 3 collaborative rounds with varying partner resource states (plentiful, near depletion, exhausted).

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Motor tile placement; differentiated from C2 (pacing) and C3 (alignment repair).

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Control trial where partner has full resources and sharing would deplete player needlessly ('more sharing != always better').

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `round_start`
- `partner_resource_state_updated`
- `resource_transfer_initiated`
- `resource_amount_selected`
- `tile_placed`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `need_sensitive_sharing_index`
- **Formula:** (sharing_when_partner_depleted - sharing_when_partner_full) / max_possible_transfer
- **Units:** index (-1 to 1)
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Positive values reflect targeted prosocial sharing attuned to genuine peer need.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Normalized differential allocation index.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `2`
- **Expected Observations:** `3`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `30000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** No canvas engagement or zero tiles handled.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Keyboard palette transfers (Number keys 1-5 to transfer / space to place).

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Fewer than 2 rounds completed. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
