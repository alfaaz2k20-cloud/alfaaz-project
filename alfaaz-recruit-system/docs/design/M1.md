# Mini-Game Design Sheet: M1 — Minimum Completed
**World:** The Repetition (`W7`)  
**Target Parameter:** `motivation`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `baseline_task_completion`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `motivation`
- **Facet:** `baseline_task_completion`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `motivation`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Baseline Execution Quality on Routine Tasks
- **Honesty Note:** Measures quality, pace consistency, and diligence during mandatory baseline repetitive units.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate formats and validates a series of repetitive cultural transcript cards (matching typography style and checking hyphenation) up to the explicit stated required minimum (3 units).
- **Trial Structure:** 3 mandatory baseline units.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Typing / verification clicks; neutral, non-flashy interface.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** Uniform difficulty across all units.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `unit_loaded`
- `formatting_applied`
- `unit_verified`
- `mandatory_minimum_reached`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `mandatory_cadence_consistency`
- **Formula:** std_dev(unit_completion_durations) / mean(unit_completion_durations)
- **Units:** coefficient of variation
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Lower variation indicates steady, disciplined execution rhythm on routine work.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Coefficient of variation of processing durations.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `3`
- **Expected Observations:** `3`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `30000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Less than 3 mandatory units completed before cutoff.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Full keyboard shortcuts for text styling/formatting toggles.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Mandatory minimum not completed. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
