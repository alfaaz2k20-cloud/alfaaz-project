# Mini-Game Design Sheet: A3 — Quality Control
**World:** The Archive (`W2`)  
**Target Parameter:** `conscientiousness`  
**Behavioral Facet:** `self_correction_and_verification`  

---

## 1. Target Parameter & Behavioral Facet
- **Parameter Key:** `conscientiousness`
- **Facet:** `self_correction_and_verification`
- **Definition Reference:** Bound to authoritative definition in `config/parameters.json` for `conscientiousness`.

## 2. Nearest Researched Construct & Honest Match Note
- **Nearest Construct:** Post-Task Auditing & Error Correction
- **Honesty Note:** Measures voluntary verification behavior before final archival submission.

## 3. Task Description & Trial Structure
- **Description:** Candidate is presented with a summary ledger of 6 previously filed items (2 of which contain injected transcript errors) and given unpressured opportunity to review, verify, or correct.
- **Trial Structure:** 1 comprehensive ledger review phase with 6 item rows.

## 4. Nuisance Demands & Sibling Differentiation
- **Nuisance Demands:** Visual comparison of side-by-side strings; distinguishes careful accurate work without checking from heavy checking with poor error detection.

## 5. Control / Decoy Conditions
- **Controls:** 4 clean records that require no changes.

## 6. Raw Events Logged
- `ledger_opened`
- `row_inspected`
- `edit_modal_triggered`
- `correction_applied`
- `ledger_finalized`

## 7. Extracted Behavioral Features
### Feature: `error_detection_sensitivity`
- **Formula:** count(true errors corrected) / total_true_errors
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Sensitivity to discrepancies in catalogued records.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Signal detection sensitivity (d' or hit rate).

### Feature: `false_alarm_rate`
- **Formula:** count(clean records erroneously altered) / total_clean_records
- **Units:** ratio
- **Direction of Interpretation:** Distinguishes disciplined checking from indiscriminate editing.
- **Construct Distinction (Why not click count):** Error rate metric.



## 8. Data Sufficiency (`min_observations`)
- **Minimum Observations for `USABLE` Status:** `1`
- **Expected Observations:** `1`

## 9. Time Ceiling (`ceiling_ms`) & Censoring
- **Ceiling:** `35000 ms`
- **Behavior on Ceiling:** Task gracefully concludes; logged with `stop_reason: "ceiling"`; observations are marked right-censored.

## 10. Validity Rules (`INVALID` Criteria)
- **Invalidation Condition:** Finalized instantaneously (<500ms) or corrupted ledger state.

## 11. Accessibility Alternative & Feature Exclusion
- **Interaction Alternative:** Accessible data table markup with aria-sort and keyboard focusable cells.

## 12. Insufficient Evidence Manifestation
- **Insufficient Condition:** Ledger bypassed without inspection. Result marked `INSUFFICIENT` (never `LOW`).

## 13. Proposed Elements
- **PROPOSED (needs owner approval):** All heuristic features and thresholds are uncalibrated (`null` thresholds in `config/feature_bands.json`) pending empirical normative volunteer data.
